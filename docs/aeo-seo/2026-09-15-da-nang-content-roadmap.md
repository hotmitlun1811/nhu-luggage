# Da Nang Content Roadmap — 12 broad travel-authority articles

**Status:** Proposed plan, awaiting owner sign-off (2026-09-15). No articles written yet.
**Goal:** ~12 high-traffic tourist articles that build travel + domain authority and internal-link back to the luggage-storage money pages. Owner chose: **broad travel-authority focus**, **focused set of ~12**, deep + cited standard.
**Method:** intent + competitor mapping via free methods only (Google autocomplete, People-Also-Ask, related searches, competitor content inventories, forums). No Semrush. Full research trail: `scratchpad/research/da-nang-content-roadmap-research.md`.

**Every article follows the house standard already shipped for the 5 guides:** ~2,000–2,600 words, TL;DR answer block, sticky-TOC layout, question-shaped H2s, comparison tables, cited Sources, plain native/global English, không-dấu Vietnamese names + "VND", the bottom booking form (`#reserve`), and per-article deep research (6–8+ sources) with an outline reviewed before writing.

**The one gap that ties it all together:** every incumbent (hoiandaytrip, ahoyvietnam, TripAdvisor, the big blogs) writes attractions and hotels but ignores the universal *logistics moment* — landed before check-in, checked out before a night flight, a big bag that won't fit on a GrabBike, a beach day with nowhere to leave your things. That moment is Stow's home turf, and it lets a broad tourist article convert without feeling salesy.

## The recommended 12 (writing sequence)

For each: primary keyword · the real questions it answers · the gap we beat · the tactful Stow tie-in · internal links.

**Wave 1 — logistics-strong, most winnable (write first)**

1. **Da Nang 3-Day Itinerary** (with 1/2/4/5-day options)
   `da nang itinerary 3 days` · "How many days are enough in Da Nang?" · Gap: nobody plans the arrival-day (land 9am, check-in 2pm) and departure-day (checkout 12pm, flight 8pm) dead time · Tie-in: Day 1 drop bags near DAD before check-in; Day 3 one last beach + meal bag-free · Links: layover, marble-mountains, hoi-an, ba-na guides + `#booking`. *Interim cluster hub.*

2. **Where to Stay in Da Nang** (neighborhoods, not a hotel listicle)
   `where to stay in da nang` · "Best area to stay?", "Da Nang or Hoi An?" · Gap: incumbents rank hotels, none solve the early-check-in / late-checkout gap by area · Tie-in: "Most An Thuong hotels check in at 2pm — land early, store bags, hit the beach" · Links: `#booking`, #6 (Da Nang vs Hoi An), hoi-an guide.

3. **Da Nang Airport (DAD) Guide** (arrivals, SIM, to the city / Hoi An)
   `da nang airport guide` · "How far is Hoi An from the airport?" · Gap: no one ties the airport to a nearby bag-drop for early arrivals / long layovers · Tie-in: most on-brand query — Stow is 10 min from DAD · Links: layover guide, #4, hoi-an guide, `#booking`.

4. **Getting Around Da Nang** (Grab, motorbike, taxi, transfers)
   `how to get around da nang` · "Is Grab safe in Da Nang?" · Gap: nobody flags that a big suitcase won't fit on a GrabBike · Tie-in: "leave the big bag at Stow, scoot the city with a daypack" · Links: #3, layover guide, `#booking`.

5. **Da Nang Food Guide** (what to eat + where)
   `what to eat in da nang` / `da nang food` · "What is Da Nang famous for?" · Gap: most are photo lists; own the hands-free food-tour / printable dish-map framing · Tie-in: "don't carry luggage between stalls on a Han Market crawl" · Links: #1, layover guide, #12. *Top authority/link magnet.*

6. **Da Nang vs Hoi An** (where to base yourself)
   `da nang or hoi an` · "Is it nicer to stay in Da Nang or Hoi An?" · Gap: none cover the logistics of the swap day / day-tripping one from the other · Tie-in: "store bags on the swap day so you're not on the bus with them" · Links: hoi-an guide (primary), #2, `#booking`.

**Wave 2 — authority + reach (write next)**

7. **Best Beaches in Da Nang** (My Khe, Non Nuoc, coves)
   `best beaches in da nang` · Gap: none serve the beach-day-on-arrival/departure traveler with no hotel · Tie-in: "sand to airport — store bags, beach, fly"; Non Nuoc is next to the shop · Links: marble-mountains guide, #1, `#booking`.

8. **Best Time to Visit Da Nang** (month-by-month weather)
   `best time to visit da nang` · "Which month to avoid Da Nang?" (appears on 3 seeds) · Gap: most bury the answer; a crisp avoid-Oct–Nov table + rainy-day plan wins the snippet · Tie-in: minimal (authority/link hub) · Links: #1, ba-na guide, #9.

9. **Da Nang with Kids** (family guide)
   `da nang with kids` · Gap: family travel-day logistics (stroller, car-seat, extra bags) never covered · Tie-in: "store the bags and the stroller, let the kids nap-then-play before a night flight" · Links: ba-na + marble-mountains guides, #7, #1.

10. **Son Tra Peninsula / Lady Buddha**
    `son tra peninsula` · Gap: no tight half-day loop paired with a same-day airport departure (both near the shop) · Tie-in: "last-morning loop — bags at Stow, up to Lady Buddha, straight to DAD" · Links: #1, marble-mountains guide, #7.

11. **Da Nang to Hue via Hai Van Pass** (day trip)
    `da nang to hue day trip` · Gap: OTAs sell tours but ignore the independent option / luggage-in-Da-Nang loop · Tie-in: "leave the big bags, take a daypack over the pass"; completes the day-trip set · Links: hoi-an + ba-na guides, `#booking`.

12. **Da Nang at Night** (Dragon Bridge fire show, bars, night markets)
    `da nang nightlife` + `dragon bridge fire show` · "What time is the fire show?" (snippet bait) · Gap: nobody serves the checked-out red-eye traveler wanting one last night out · Tie-in: "check out, bags at Stow (open till 10pm), enjoy the 9pm show bag-free" · Links: #1, #5, `#booking`.

## Cluster structure (hub-and-spoke)

- **Plan Your Trip:** #1 Itinerary (interim hub), #8 Best Time → feed every spoke + `#booking`.
- **Logistics (Stow's home turf):** #3 Airport, #4 Getting Around ↔ published layover guide → strongest money-page pull.
- **Where to Stay:** #2 Neighborhoods ↔ #6 Da Nang vs Hoi An → hoi-an guide + `#booking`.
- **Eat & Night:** #5 Food ↔ #12 Nightlife → #1 + layover guide.
- **Beaches & Day Trips:** #7 Beaches, #9 Kids, #10 Son Tra, #11 Hue — mirror and cross-link the published marble-mountains / ba-na / hoi-an guides.

**Money-page rule (every article):** one contextual in-body link to the most relevant existing guide + one CTA to `#booking` (or `#pricing` on where-to-stay, `/trust-safety` on any safety angle). Storage mentions capped at 1–2 per article, always inside a real logistics moment. Plus the bottom `#reserve` booking form on every page.

## Avoid (unwinnable or off-brand)
"Best hotels in Da Nang" affiliate listicles · "flights to Da Nang" (pure OTA) · "cost of living / apartments for rent / Da Nang Dragons basketball" (off-brand autocomplete neighbours).

## Timing
Publish #8 Best Time and the beach/outdoor pieces (#7, #10) by Dec–Jan to catch dry-season (Feb–May) and beach-peak (Apr–Aug) planning traffic. State the Dragon Bridge show schedule precisely (Fri–Sun 9pm) — recurring "what time" query and snippet bait.

## Process per article (unchanged from the 5 guides)
Deep research (6–8+ sources, free methods) → outline reviewed → write to the house standard → verify (build + mobile/desktop screenshots) → commit. Ship in waves; reassess with Search Console data after Wave 1 has ~28 days live.
